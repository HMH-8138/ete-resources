❓ Frequently Asked Questions

+ How often is the routine updated?
The class routine is updated every semester and can be modified during the semester if necessary. Students are notified through email/SMS about any changes to the routine.
+ What should I do if there's a conflict in my schedule?
If you notice any scheduling conflicts, please contact the Academic Affairs Office immediately. They will help resolve the issue or provide alternative arrangements.
+ Can I download the routine as a PDF?
Yes! You can download the routine from the external link provided above. The routine is usually available in PDF and image formats for easy access and printing.
+ Where can I find lab session timings?
Lab session timings are included in the detailed routine for each level. Lab sessions typically occur on different days and may have extended durations compared to regular classes.
+ What if the routine link is not working?
If the routine link is not accessible, please contact the IT support or Academic Affairs Office. You can also check with your department for an alternative access method.
