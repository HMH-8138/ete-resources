PDF Upload Guidelines

Rules & Guidelines for Uploading PDF Files

1. File Format & Size

  • Only PDF files are accepted (.pdf extension)
  • Maximum file size: 50 MB
  • Ensure the PDF is properly formatted and readable

2. File Naming Convention

  • Use descriptive file names (e.g., "Digital-Electronics-Lecture-01.pdf")
  • Avoid special characters and spaces; use hyphens or underscores instead
  • Keep file names concise but informative

3. Content Requirements

  • Content must be relevant to the department and courses
  • All materials must be original work or properly cited
  • No copyrighted material without proper attribution
  • Ensure content is academically appropriate

4. Document Quality

  • PDFs must be clear and legible
  • Use standard fonts for better readability
  • Ensure images and diagrams are properly embedded
  • Check for spelling and grammar before uploading

5. Metadata & Documentation

  • Include title, author name, and date of creation
  • Add a brief description of the document content
  • Specify the course/semester it relates to

6. Copyright & Originality

  • Uploaded content must not infringe on copyrights
  • Give proper credits to original authors
  • Use only open-source or licensed materials
  • Personal notes and assignments are encouraged

7. Upload Process

  • Log in with your student/faculty credentials
  • Navigate to the appropriate course/semester folder
  • Click "Upload" and select your PDF file
  • Fill in all required metadata fields
  • Review before final submission

8. Moderation & Approval

  • All uploads are reviewed by the moderators
  • Content review may take 24-48 hours
  • Inappropriate content will be rejected
  • You will receive notification once approved

Questions?

If you have any questions about uploading PDFs or the guidelines, please contact the Department Office or reach out to the Portal Administrator.
